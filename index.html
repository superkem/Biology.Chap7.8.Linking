6 · The whole picture

Chloroplasts and mitochondria are partners. The chloroplast takes in light, carbon dioxide and water, and makes glucose and oxygen. The mitochondrion uses that glucose and oxygen to make ATP for cell work. Watch the diagram, then explain it in your own words.
